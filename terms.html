2. Eligibility & accounts

  • You must be at least 18 years old and able to enter into contracts.
  • Give accurate information and keep it up to date. One person, one account. Fake or duplicate accounts are removed.
  • Keep your password safe and turn on two-factor authentication. You're responsible for activity on your account.
  • We may ask you to verify your identity (VAs) or business (clients) before you can withdraw money, post certain jobs, or get badges.

3. Our role

Jobseekers.ph is a marketplace. We help clients and VAs find each other, communicate, track work, and pay and get paid. The engagement itself is a contract directly between the client and the VA. We are not an employer, agency or party to that contract, and we don't control how, when or where VAs do their work.

VAs are independent contractors. They are responsible for their own taxes (including BIR registration and filing) and their own SSS, PhilHealth and Pag-IBIG contributions. Our Contracts & Taxes tools are there to help, but they are not legal or tax advice.

4. Fees

  • Posting jobs and creating a profile are free.
  • A platform fee is deducted from VA earnings paid through Jobseekers.ph: 10% on Basic, 5% on Pro, 3% on Premium. Client plan fees and subscription prices are shown on the Pricing page.
  • Subscriptions renew automatically until cancelled. Paid client plans come with a 30-day money-back guarantee.
  • We'll give at least 30 days' notice before raising fees.

5. Payments & escrow

  • For hourly work, clients are billed for hours tracked or invoiced by the VA. Clients have 5 days to review an invoice. If they don't dispute it, it is approved automatically.
  • For fixed-price milestones and paid trials, clients fund escrow up front. Funds are released when the client approves the work, or automatically 7 days after the VA submits it if the client doesn't respond.
  • VAs can withdraw to GCash, Maya, or a Philippine bank account. Exchange rates and any provider fees are shown before you confirm a withdrawal.
  • Payment protection only covers payments made through Jobseekers.ph.

6. Paid trials

A paid trial is a short engagement of 1 to 3 days with a fixed scope agreed in writing. The trial cost is funded into escrow before the trial starts and is paid to the VA when the scope is delivered. Either party may decide not to continue after a trial, with no notice period. Trial work belongs to the client once it is paid.

7. Replacement Guarantee

If a VA hired through Jobseekers.ph resigns, stops responding for 3 or more working days, or doesn't show up within 30 days of the contract start date, the client can request a replacement. We will provide a shortlist of 3 to 5 suitable VAs within 48 hours, at no extra hiring fee. It doesn't apply when the client ends the contract for reasons unrelated to the VA's conduct or availability, or when the hire was arranged off-platform.

8. Staying on the platform

For 24 months after first connecting on Jobseekers.ph, clients and VAs agree to pay and get paid for work with each other through Jobseekers.ph, unless the client pays the opt-out fee shown in their account. Asking someone to pay or communicate outside the platform to avoid fees is a breach of these Terms. It also removes payment protection, dispute help, and the Replacement Guarantee.

9. Prohibited conduct

You may not:

  • Charge VAs any fee, deposit, or "training" or "registration" cost to apply, be hired, or receive work
  • Require VAs to buy equipment, software or materials from you
  • Post fake, misleading, or illegal jobs, including recruitment for scams, money mules, crypto schemes, or adult content
  • Ask for OTPs, bank passwords, or government ID outside our verification flow
  • Harass, discriminate against, or threaten anyone
  • Misrepresent skills, test results, setup, or identity, or have someone else do your work or assessments
  • Scrape the site, spam users, or try to get around our security or scam filters

Our systems flag suspected violations automatically, and our Trust & Safety team reviews them. Breaking these rules can lead to post removal, suspension, or a permanent ban, and we may report illegal activity to authorities.

10. Badges & verification

Badges such as Verified, Brownout-ready, Academy certificates and Trust Scores are based on information and tests available to us at the time. They help with decisions but are not guarantees of future performance. We may re-check badges at any time and remove them if the information was false or is no longer accurate. Brownout-ready badges are valid for 90 days.

11. Content & work ownership

  • You keep ownership of what you post (profiles, portfolios, job posts) and let us display it on Jobseekers.ph to run the service.
  • Unless the client and VA agree otherwise in writing, work a VA creates for a client belongs to the client once it is paid for.
  • Don't post anything you don't have the rights to.

12. Disputes

If a client and VA can't agree on payment or work quality, either can open a dispute within 30 days of the invoice or milestone. Our team reviews messages, time logs, and delivered work, and may release funds to the VA, refund the client, or split the amount. We aim to decide within 10 working days. Our decision covers escrowed funds only and doesn't stop either party from pursuing other legal remedies.

13. Suspension & closing

We may suspend or close accounts that break these Terms, put others at risk, or are required to be closed by law. We'll tell you why unless the law or safety prevents it, and you can appeal by emailing support@jobseekers.ph.
