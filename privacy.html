Privacy Policy

The short version
  • We collect what we need to verify users, match VAs with clients, and process payments.
  • We never sell your personal data.
  • Work Tools screenshots are off by default. VAs can delete any screenshot before it's sent.

  • We follow the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) and the rules of the National Privacy Commission (NPC).

1. Who we are

Jobseekers.ph ("we", "us") runs an online marketplace that connects Filipino virtual assistants ("VAs") with clients around the world. We are the personal information controller for the data described in this policy. Our office is at 8F, Cebu Business Park Tower, Cebu City, 6000 Philippines.

ID documents and payment details are sensitive personal information under the Data Privacy Act. We collect them only for verification, payments, and legal compliance, and we limit who can access them.

3. How we use it

  • Create and run your account, and show your profile to the right people
  • Match VAs and clients (including AI Match, shift matching, and search)
  • Verify identities, businesses, certificates and Brownout-ready badges
  • Process payments, escrow, paid trials, payouts and invoices
  • Detect and stop scams, fake job posts, and off-platform payment requests, including automated checks on job posts and messages
  • Handle disputes and Replacement Guarantee requests
  • Send service messages (for example: new applicant, trial accepted, invoice paid). Marketing emails are opt-in and you can unsubscribe at any time
  • Meet legal duties, such as tax records and responding to lawful requests from authorities

Automated checks only flag content. A person on our Trust & Safety team makes the final call before an account is suspended, and you can ask for a review.

4. Legal basis

Under Sections 12 and 13 of the Data Privacy Act, we process your data because it is needed for our contract with you, to comply with the law, for our legitimate interests (such as fraud prevention and platform security), or with your consent (for example, marketing emails and optional setup photos). Where we rely on consent, you can withdraw it at any time.

5. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only when needed:

  • Other users: clients see VA profiles and applications; VAs see a client's company, trust score and job details. ID documents are never shown to other users, only the "Verified" badge.
  • Service providers under written data-sharing or outsourcing agreements: payment processors and e-wallets, ID verification, cloud hosting, email delivery, and customer support tools.
  • Authorities, when required by law, court order, or to protect someone's safety.
  • A buyer or successor if Jobseekers.ph is merged or sold, with the same protections in place.

6. International transfers

Many clients and some of our service providers are outside the Philippines. When data leaves the country, we stay accountable for it under Section 21 of the Data Privacy Act and use contracts that require the same level of protection.

7. How long we keep it

  • Account and profile data: while your account is open, then up to 2 years after closing (to handle disputes and fraud checks)
  • ID verification images: deleted within 90 days after verification; we keep only the result
  • Payment, invoice and tax records: for as long as Philippine tax and accounting laws require
  • Work Tools screenshots: 90 days, unless part of an open dispute
  • Messages: while the account is open, or longer if needed for a dispute or legal claim

8. Security & breaches

We use encryption in transit and at rest, role-based access, two-factor authentication for staff, and regular security reviews. If a personal data breach is likely to put you at real risk, we will notify the National Privacy Commission and affected users within 72 hours of knowing about it, as required by NPC rules.

9. Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act, you have the right to:

  • Be informed about how your data is processed
  • Access your data and get a copy
  • Correct wrong or outdated data
  • Erase or block data that is no longer needed or was processed unlawfully
  • Object to processing, including marketing and automated decisions
  • Data portability: get your data in a common electronic format
  • Damages and to file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph)

Most of this can be done from Settings. For anything else, contact our Data Protection Officer. We reply within 15 working days.

10. Cookies & local storage

We use essential cookies and browser storage to keep you logged in, remember your language (English or Taglish), and save drafts such as unfinished applications or time logs. With your consent, we also use analytics cookies to understand which pages help people most. You can block non-essential cookies in your browser at any time.

11. Age requirement

You must be at least 18 years old to use Jobseekers.ph. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If we learn that a minor has signed up, we close the account and delete the data.

12. Changes

If we make important changes, we will tell you by email or with a notice on the site at least 15 days before they take effect. The date at the top shows the latest version.
