Our Story

We started with a simple belief

In 2020, our founder — a Filipino entrepreneur who had experienced both the frustration of finding quality remote talent and the difficulty of breaking into the global market — decided something had to change.

Filipino virtual assistants are among the most skilled, dedicated, and English-proficient remote workers in the world. Yet there was no platform built specifically for them — one that understood the Philippine context, celebrated Filipino talent, and connected them directly with the clients who needed them most.

Jobseekers.ph was born from that vision. Today, we're proud to be the Philippines' #1 VA marketplace, having helped over 12,000 virtual assistants find meaningful, well-paying remote work — and helped thousands of global businesses find the incredible support they needed to grow.

Our Values

What we stand for

Every decision we make is guided by these core principles.

Trust & Transparency

We believe in honest communication and clear expectations — from pricing to platform policies. No hidden fees, no surprises, no fine print games.

People First

Behind every profile is a real person with real ambitions. We design every feature to genuinely improve the lives of VAs and the businesses they support.

Excellence Always

We set a high bar for quality on both sides. Every VA is verified, every job posting is reviewed, and every transaction is secured.

Empowerment

We empower Filipino VAs to build sustainable careers on their own terms — choosing their clients, setting their rates, and working when they want.

Global Mindset

We bridge the gap between Filipino talent and global opportunity. Geography should never be a barrier to great work or fair pay.

Community

We're more than a marketplace. We're a community of remote workers supporting each other with resources, mentorship, and shared success.
