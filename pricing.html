Pricing FAQ

Yes! You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. When you upgrade, you're charged the prorated difference. When you downgrade, the new rate applies at the next billing cycle.
We accept all major credit/debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex), PayPal, and bank transfers. For enterprise plans, we also support invoicing.
The platform fee is deducted from the VA's earnings, not added to what you pay. So if a VA charges $10/hr and you pay $10/hr, the VA receives $9/hr (on the Basic plan). This keeps costs transparent for clients.
You set a fixed scope for 1–3 days and the trial cost is held in escrow. When the VA finishes, you approve and they get paid. Then you decide whether to continue. If it's not a fit, you part ways with no notice period.
If your VA resigns, stops responding, or doesn't show up within 30 days of the start date, we send a free shortlist of replacements within 48 hours. It doesn't cover ending the contract yourself for reasons unrelated to the VA (like a budget cut), but you can always post again for free.
